I'm Queen Medusa

I started this channel because I was tired of washed-out encodes and subtitles that read like machine translation.

Now I upload anime and donghua in 4K, FHD and HD, and I read every subtitle line before it goes out. The aim is simple: a file you can press play on and forget about.

Telegram is where releases land for everyone. Discord is where you can request a title, get early access, and tell me when a line sounds wrong.

  • The cleanest source

    I pick the best source available and check it for problems before encoding starts.

  • Encodes that hold up

    HEVC 10-bit for 4K and FHD to keep banding down, plus a smaller HD version for slow connections.

  • Subtitles read by a person

    Timing and typesetting are fixed by hand. Signs and songs get proper styling, and the subs are soft so you can switch tracks.

  • Delivery

    Donors on Discord get new episodes first. Everyone else gets them on Telegram shortly after.
